04 — FAQ

Clear answers

Straight facts about the experience, the stack, and how to engage Nexus Studio.

What is NEXUS // 3D?

NEXUS // 3D is a liquid-glass WebGL sculpture choreographed by scroll. It presents four cinematic acts inside one continuous engine for immersive product launches.

What technology powers NEXUS // 3D?

The experience runs on Vite with vanilla JavaScript modules, Three.js for WebGL rendering, GSAP ScrollTrigger for camera choreography, and Lenis for momentum-based smooth scrolling.

How does the scroll-driven camera choreography work?

A master GSAP ScrollTrigger timeline maps page scroll to four acts: macro close-up, a 120° orbital sweep, a dive into the mesh with noise warp, then a pass through the core into the terminal interface.

How do I start a project with Nexus Studio?

Use the contact form or email hello@nexus3d.studio. Share your product, launch goals, and desired feeling — Nexus Studio replies within two business days.
