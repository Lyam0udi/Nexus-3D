FAQ

What is NEXUS // 3D?

A liquid-glass WebGL sculpture choreographed by scroll — five cinematic acts inside one continuous engine for immersive product launches.

What powers it?

Vite, vanilla ES modules, Three.js, GSAP ScrollTrigger, and Lenis — no React rewrite, no secret env required to run the demo.

How does the camera choreography work?

A master scrubbed timeline maps scroll to five acts: square presence, orbit, threshold into a digital interior, then emergence of the architectural core before contact.
